# Preserve the Mini App design

Keep the existing navy dark palette, light palette, card geometry, typography,
icons, full-height background and complete catalogue/subscription sections.
Preserve light/dark/system selection before loading and after refresh. Keep the
selected control paint contained, respect reduced motion, and retain scroll and
focus during in-place updates. Do not add directional rays or restyle controls
as part of functional fixes. Ordinary bug fixes need no additional design approval.

visual changes must never create purchase consent or unconfirmed paid access.
